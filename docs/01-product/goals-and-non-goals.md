# Goals and non-goals

## Goals

- Independently implement property creation, unit creation, listing drafting, first review and publication.
- Search eligible units for a complete date interval, with canonical amenities and resolved policies.
- Save authenticated users' favourite **properties**, persist hearts and counts, and protect private saved data.
- Keep enquiries PENDING during phone/offline discussion; ACCEPT only with successful proposal creation.
- Require exact-contract mutual acknowledgement, retain submitted versions, hold dates temporarily and enforce nonoverlap in PostgreSQL.
- Work through GitHub issues and reviewed PRs, with reproducible migrations, seed data and tests.

## Non-goals

No phone OTP, KYC, in-app chat/calendar/viewings, payments, legal e-signature engine, confirmed mid-term amendments, property/image reapproval or AI-generated thumbnails. No Kafka/RabbitMQ/Elasticsearch, background workers, OpenAPI, logging/monitoring or caching work. Maps, geocoding, Google login and property reviews retain P2 domain status unless scope is explicitly changed. Do not infer features from prototype language buttons, notification badges or map buttons alone.
